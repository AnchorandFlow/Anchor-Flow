Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 17, 2026

We built Anchor & Flow for families — which means your data is yours, full stop. We don't sell it, we don't share it, and we only collect what we need to make the app work for you.

Who we are

Anchor & Flow is a family home management app created and operated by Lindsey Border. If you have questions about this policy, contact us at hello@anchorandflowapp.com.

What information we collect

We collect only what's necessary to run the app:

  • Account information — your email address, display name, and encrypted password when you create an account
  • Household data — tasks, meals, calendar events, shopping lists, and other content you create inside the app
  • Family profile — optional information you choose to share (family size, dietary needs, schedules) to personalise AI suggestions
  • Google Calendar data — if you connect Google Calendar, we access your event titles, dates, and times to display them in the app. We do not store your Google Calendar data on our servers — it is fetched live and held in your browser only
  • Usage data — basic information about how you use the app to help us improve it (no third-party analytics trackers)

How we use your information

  • To provide and personalise the Anchor & Flow experience for your family
  • To generate AI-powered suggestions, daily briefings, and notifications using your household data
  • To sync your data across devices and household members
  • To send you notifications you've opted into (morning anchor, midday check-in, evening recap)
  • To communicate with you about your account or the service

AI features and your data

Anchor & Flow uses Claude (by Anthropic) to power features like "Build my day", daily suggestions, and notifications. When you use these features, relevant household data (tasks, meals, calendar events) is sent to Anthropic's API to generate a response. This data is used only to generate your response and is not used to train AI models.

Anthropic's privacy policy applies to data processed through their API: anthropic.com/privacy

Data storage and security

Your account and household data is stored securely using Supabase, a trusted database platform. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We use industry-standard security practices and regularly review our systems.

Your household data is protected by Row Level Security (RLS) — only authenticated members of your household can access your data.

Family sharing

When you invite a family member to your household using a household code, they will be able to see and edit all shared household data (tasks, meals, calendar events, shopping lists). Only share your household code with people you trust.

Google Calendar

If you connect Google Calendar, we request read-only access to your calendar events. We do not modify, delete, or store your Google Calendar data. You can disconnect Google Calendar at any time from the Calendar tab in the app, which immediately removes all synced events.

Data retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time by emailing hello@anchorandflowapp.com. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

Children's privacy

Anchor & Flow is designed for parents and adult household members. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Notifications

If you enable browser notifications, we use your device's notification system to send you AI-generated daily briefings and reminders. You can disable notifications at any time through your browser or device settings, or from the Settings tab in the app.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Export your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@anchorandflowapp.com.

Third-party services

We use the following third-party services to operate Anchor & Flow:

  • Supabase — database and authentication (privacy policy)
  • Anthropic (Claude) — AI features (privacy policy)
  • Vercel — app hosting (privacy policy)
  • Google — optional calendar integration (privacy policy)

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we do, we'll update the date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we'll notify you by email or through the app.

Contact us

Questions about this privacy policy or how we handle your data? We'd love to hear from you.
